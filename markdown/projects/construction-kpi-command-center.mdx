Construction KPI Command Center is a dashboard product that pulls project data from spreadsheets and platform exports and turns it into a live view of performance. It includes automated daily and weekly reporting, executive summaries, and drill down pages for project managers and supers. The focus is consistency, less manual reporting, and faster detection of schedule and cost issues.
