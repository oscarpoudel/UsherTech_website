PocketScan Site Mapper is a field ready Android application for scanning rooms and corridors and converting them into a usable map. The workflow targets fast capture during walkthroughs, producing a floorplan style output plus a simplified 3D model that can be exported and shared with the project team. The app supports tagging areas and notes during capture, then synchronizes results to a cloud workspace for review, versioning, and distribution.
