RFI and Submittal AutoDraft is a small toolset that converts informal notes and photos into clean RFI and submittal drafts. It standardizes tone and formatting, auto fills common fields, and creates consistent attachments and backup documentation packages. It is designed to reduce the time spent rewriting field observations while improving traceability and completeness.
