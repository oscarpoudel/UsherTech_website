BuildBIM is a BIM authoring app: parametric walls, doors and windows on levels and grids, with views, schedules, sheets and clash checks from one model. Your files stay on your computer and the app runs locally.

## Highlights

- Parametric walls, doors, and windows placed on levels and grids
- Views, schedules, sheets, and clash checks generated from one model
- Runs locally with Node 20.19+; no packages required
- Available for browser, Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, and iPad

## Getting started

```sh
node devshell/serve.mjs
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000/ in your browser.
