AffordabilityOS turns parcel intake, zoning checks, 485-x readiness, underwriting, files, and AI-guided diligence into one review-ready workspace for NYC affordable housing acquisition decisions.

## Platform

- Parcel-to-decision workflow: resolve address or BBL, pull public parcel facts, estimate capacity, screen incentive fit, compare scenarios, export investor-ready diligence reports
- AI copilot with tool results that explains risks and drafts checklists
- Scenario finance engine comparing market-rate, mixed-income, and affordable scenarios (TDC, NOI, DSCR, return on cost, funding gap, IRR, NPV, residual land value)
- Cited compliance package with review flags, professional-review notes, source ledgers, and PDF/Markdown feasibility memos

Outputs require professional review before reliance.
